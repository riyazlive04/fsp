## 20. FAQ

### Who can join FSP?

FSP is open to aspiring trainers, new trainers, experienced trainers,
facilitators and HR/L&D professionals.

### Do I need previous training experience?

No. Beginners can start with the foundation.

### Is FSP only about facilitation?

No. FSP covers facilitation, experiential learning, training design,
personal branding, marketing, proposals and professional growth.

### Is FSP online or offline?

FSP includes different learning experiences. The format depends on the
specific program or batch.

### Is there a community?

Yes. Community is an important part of the FSP ecosystem.

### What is the 30 Days Challenge?

It is a practical 30-day journey where participants complete one focused
task each day.

### What is Good to Great Facilitator?

It is the FSP certification journey designed around learning, practice,
reflection and continuous improvement.

### Will I get resources?

Yes. FSP provides practical resources, templates, activities and
learning materials.

### How do I join FSP?

Click Join FSP and register your interest. The FSP team will contact you
with the relevant program details.
